Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy applies to the Google OAuth application Jarvis, a private personal-application client of the Yates household. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

When a household member signs in, the application is granted access to that person's own Google account: Gmail (read and send), Calendar, Drive and Docs (files and folders the assistant manages), Spreadsheets, and Contacts (read). No data is accessed beyond the signed-in account.

How the data is used

Data is processed on the family's own computer in the household, by the assistant, to carry out household tasks: email, calendar, documents, and smart-home control. Data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Storage

Records kept by the assistant (email logs, task state, notes) are stored locally on the family's hardware under operating-system access controls.

Your controls

You can revoke the application's access at any time: Google account → Security → Third-party access, then remove "Jarvis." Deleting the application's Google Cloud project revokes all access immediately.
