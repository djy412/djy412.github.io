Yates Home — Jarvis

Home of the software behind Jarvis, the personal AI assistant for the Yates household. The linked Google OAuth application ("Jarvis") is a private, single-household client that lets the assistant read and send the household's Gmail, work with the household's Calendar, Drive, and Contacts, and coordinate smart-home devices. It runs on the family's own hardware, is built and operated by the Yates family, and is not a commercial service.
